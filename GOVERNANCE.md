# AXION Governance Principles

## Human-Final Authority

Humans make decisions.

AI provides analysis and decision support.

---

## Explainability

Every assessment should be understandable.

---

## Auditability

Every decision should be reviewable.

---

## Transparency

Decision processes should be visible and traceable.

---

## Accountability

Responsibility remains with authorized decision-makers.

---

## Governance-First Design

Governance is designed into the system from the beginning, not added afterward.
